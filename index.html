Hello — I'm glad you found your way here.

I work on how autonomous systems perceive the world around them: a handful of questions about fusing noisy sensor data into decisions a robot or vehicle can be trusted to make. My Master's research on high-level object fusion for self-driving cars, done at TU Dortmund's Institute of Control Theory and Systems Engineering, turned into a paper I still enjoy explaining to people outside the field.

These days I'm a Robotics Engineer focused on perception at Ant Robotics, building the sensing and fusion pipelines that let our robots understand their surroundings.

I also build small open-source tools when the software I need doesn't exist, and I write for people outside the field whenever I can manage it. If any of that overlaps with what you're doing, I'd like to hear from you.
